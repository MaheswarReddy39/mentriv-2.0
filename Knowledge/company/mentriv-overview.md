# Mentriv Overview

## What is Mentriv?

Mentriv is an EdTech learning platform designed to provide students with structured, course-based online learning and mentorship.

The platform brings learning activities such as classes, assignments, MCQ tests, and progress tracking together in one place.

## Purpose

Mentriv aims to provide students with a structured learning environment where they can:

- Learn through organized courses and classes
- Complete assignments and assessments
- Track their learning progress
- Receive guidance and mentorship

## Target Users

### Students

Students use Mentriv to learn through courses, attend classes, complete assignments and assessments, and track their progress.

### Teachers / Faculty

Teachers and faculty provide learning activities such as classes and assignments and work with student submissions.

### Administrators

Administrators manage the platform and its learning operations.

## Main Platform Capabilities

Mentriv provides:

- Course-based learning
- Classes and lessons
- Assignments and submissions
- MCQ tests and assessments
- Course progress tracking
- Notifications
- Announcements
- Student, teacher, and administrative workflows

## Learning Structure

Courses are the central part of the Mentriv learning platform.

A course can contain:

- Curriculum
- Projects
- Classes and lessons
- Assignments
- MCQ tests
- Progress tracking

## Mentorship

Mentorship is part of the Mentriv learning experience and is intended to provide students with additional guidance during their learning journey.

## Platform Overview

Mentriv is a web-based learning platform with separate experiences for students, teachers, and administrators.

Students focus on learning and course activities, teachers focus on teaching and student learning activities, and administrators manage the overall platform.
