# Mentriv Mentorship

## Overview

Mentriv includes one-to-one mentorship as part of its learning experience.

Mentorship complements structured course learning and supports students alongside other learning activities such as assignments, quizzes, interview questions, and mock interviews.

## Mentors

Mentriv teachers and faculty serve as mentors for students.

## Students

Students receive mentorship support as part of their learning experience.

## Mentorship Format

Mentriv provides one-to-one mentorship, allowing students to interact individually with a mentor.

## Role in Learning

Mentorship is designed to complement course-based learning and provide additional support to students throughout their learning journey.
