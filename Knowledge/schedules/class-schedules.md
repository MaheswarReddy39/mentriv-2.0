# Mentriv Class Schedule

## Current Class Timings

Mentriv classes are currently conducted within flexible time slots based on student availability.

The currently used time slots include:

- 6:00 PM – 7:30 PM
- 8:00 PM – 9:30 PM
- 8:30 PM – 10:00 PM

## Flexible Scheduling

Mentriv class timings and schedules are not permanently fixed.

Class timings are arranged flexibly to accommodate students' availability.

The applicable class timing may vary depending on the current schedule and student availability.

## Schedule Notifications

Students receive information about their class timings and schedules through email notifications.

Students should refer to the latest schedule notification for their applicable class timing.
