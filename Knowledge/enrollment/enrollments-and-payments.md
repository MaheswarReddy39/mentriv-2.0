# Mentriv Enrollment and Payments

## Student Registration

Students can visit the Mentriv website, browse available courses, and select a course they want to enroll in.

The student selects the Enroll option and completes the registration form with the required details.

Before the registration is submitted, the student must confirm the registration.

After successful registration, the student account is created with a pending status and requires Admin approval.

## Course Enrollment

When a student registers for a course, an enrollment record is created for the selected course.

The enrollment initially has a pending status.

Admin approval is required before the student is accepted.

When the Admin approves the registration, the student's status becomes accepted and the corresponding enrollment is approved.

If the Admin rejects the registration, the student's registration and enrollment are rejected.

Students receive an email notification when their registration is approved or rejected.

## Payments

Payment is part of the Mentriv enrollment process.

The student completes the payment-related step during registration before submitting the final registration.

Mentriv provides payment management through the Admin dashboard.

## Payment Verification

Payment information submitted by students is managed through the Admin payment management area.

The specific payment method, payment account details, and complete verification procedure should be based on the current payment instructions provided by Mentriv.

## Course Access

Students must receive the required approval before they can access their Mentriv account and enrolled course.
