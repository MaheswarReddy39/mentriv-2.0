# Mentriv Policies and Rules

## Student Registration

Students must complete the registration process before participating in the student learning experience.

After registration, the student account remains pending until Admin approval.

Students must wait for Admin approval before their account is approved.

Students receive email notifications about the status of their registration.

## Course Participation

Students must be enrolled in a course to participate in its course-related learning activities.

No specific attendance requirement or minimum engagement requirement has been established.

## Assignments

Assignments are associated with specific courses and must be submitted by students.

When a new assignment is created for a course, students receive a notification.

The current assignment notification states that students have 12 hours to complete and submit the assignment.

No additional late-submission rules or penalties have been established.

## MCQ Tests

MCQ tests are associated with specific courses.

Tests may have a defined duration and passing score.

Students receive results based on their test performance.

No specific rules regarding maximum attempts, retakes, or test conduct have been established.

## Payments

Payment is part of the course enrollment process.

Payment information is reviewed through the Admin payment management system.

No specific payment deadline, cancellation rule, or refund policy has been established.

## Cancellation and Refunds

Enrollment cancellation is supported by the platform.

No specific cancellation procedure has been established.

No specific Mentriv refund policy has been established.

## Course Completion

Course progress includes completed lessons, assignments, and MCQs.

No specific completion percentage, attendance threshold, or minimum test score has been established as a general course completion requirement.

## General Platform Rules

Mentriv uses separate roles for students, teachers, admins, and super admins.

Administrative approval is part of the account and enrollment process.

Students are expected to follow the applicable course and platform requirements.
